A note about Morrow

Make a little room
for your own pace.

Morrow is a small, quiet corner for the things you’re figuring out: plans in progress, passing thoughts, and ideas worth keeping.

01

Keep it simple

No complicated systems to maintain. Just a place to gather what matters and find it again when you need it.

02

Let things take time

Not every thought needs to become a plan. Some ideas are better left to sit for a while.

03

Start where you are

A note, a list, a small next step. There’s no right way to begin making space for your thoughts.
